# Portfolio Review

## Overall Rating: 8.7/10

Johannes January's analytics portfolio is strong, recruiter-friendly, and clearly positioned for Business Analyst, Data Analyst, BI, and Sales & Operations Analytics roles. The site already communicates a credible blend of graduate analytics training, hands-on operations experience, and applied project work.

The portfolio is not just a visual website. It works as a career evidence system: role fit, proof points, coursework, selected projects, skills, resume access, and contact information all support the same professional story.

## Key Strengths

- Clear target-role positioning for analytics, BI, sales operations, and business analysis.
- Strong opening message that connects business analytics with real operations experience.
- Credibility signals are visible early, including GPA, coursework depth, completed credits, and years of experience.
- Project cards use a strong case-study structure: summary, visual story, use case, metric, focus, business value, problem, role, methods, and result.
- The downloadable resume and contact section make recruiter follow-through easy.
- The design feels polished, professional, and more premium than a typical student portfolio.

## Highest-Impact Improvements

- Add artifact links for each project, such as GitHub repositories, Tableau Public dashboards, reports, slide decks, or PDFs.
- Add quantified project outcomes where possible, especially around model performance, dashboard findings, dataset size, and business decision value.
- Add a concise work authorization line if it supports the target application strategy.
- Add social sharing metadata, including Open Graph title, description, and preview image.
- Add a small project evidence checklist so each project has at least one proof artifact.

## Quick Wins

- Add a `View Evidence` or `Project Artifact` link inside each project dropdown.
- Add one sentence near the project section explaining that artifacts are being added progressively.
- Add the portfolio link to LinkedIn Featured, LinkedIn Contact Info, and the resume header.
- Keep the one-page resume as the primary downloadable resume for recruiter speed.

## Final Assessment

This portfolio is already strong enough to share with recruiters, professors, alumni, and hiring managers. The next jump from very good to excellent will come from adding direct evidence links for each project and tightening the measurable outcomes behind the project stories.

With those additions, this can move from an 8.7/10 to roughly a 9.3/10 recruiter-ready portfolio.
